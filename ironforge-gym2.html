Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Do you offer a free trial? +
Yes! Your first full week is completely free — no strings attached, no credit card required. Just walk in or book via WhatsApp.
What are your gym timings? +
Monday to Friday: 5:00 AM – 11:00 PM. Saturday and Sunday: 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM. Elite members get 24/7 access.
Do I need prior experience to join? +
Absolutely not! We welcome complete beginners. Our trainers will assess your fitness level and create a program perfect for where you are right now.
Can I change my membership plan? +
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect from the next billing cycle with no penalties.
Are nutrition plans included? +
Basic nutrition guidance is included in the Pro plan, while advanced personalized meal plans are part of Elite membership. Standalone coaching is also available.
Do you offer group classes? +
Yes! We offer 15+ group classes per week including HIIT, Yoga, Boxing, Zumba, and CrossFit. Included in Pro and Elite plans.
